ANIMATION AND SOUND DIRECTION
- Preserve every character’s original appearance throughout all shots.
- Keep feet planted correctly during idle poses. During walking, synchronize foot contact and movement to prevent sliding.
- Keep characters grounded beside the tree and on village paths. Avoid floating, drifting, clipping or accidental changes in scale.
- Make the monster close-up unmistakably Pinky’s point of view, with the enemies looking down toward her.
- Build emotion through camera angles, timing, body language and sound.
- Use genuine pauses and small vocal reactions where appropriate. Do not speak punctuation or instructions such as “sigh” or “laugh.”
- Deliver English dialogue clearly, without rushing or artificially speeding up the voices.
- Transition the music from peaceful village warmth → playful suspense → heroic action → heartfelt celebration → anticipation.
- Keep game UI readable and use actual app screens wherever available.
Overall feeling: A peaceful little village, an unexpected threat, an unlikely heroine—and an adventure that has only just begun.
