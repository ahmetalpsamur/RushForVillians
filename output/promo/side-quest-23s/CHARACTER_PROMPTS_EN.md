# Character scene production prompts

These are production instructions, not generated clips. Prefer compositing existing sprite animations. Reference inputs must be supplied to any generation tool; text alone is insufficient to preserve the designs.

## Shared direction

Vertical 9:16, 1080 × 1920. Warm, playful pixel-art world consistent with the supplied Rush for Villains assets. Use the exact supplied Pinky and Mavili sprites: preserve palette, silhouette, proportions, face, and all identifying features. Nearest-neighbor scaling, stable character pivots. Express acting through existing idle, walk and jump poses, tiny turns and pauses; no invented limbs or facial details. No third speaking character. No generated text, captions, logos, app UI, counters, rewards or gameplay. All typography and authentic app recordings will be composited in the editor. Keep critical action away from the right edge and lower caption area. No AR camera view, tracking reticle or location-map mechanics. Dialogue is separately recorded and mixed; do not generate embedded voices or music.

## A — opening, 0–2 seconds

An explicitly fictional pixel-art park vignette. Pinky is already moving in frame one, about to set off on her daily walk. She stops and turns toward the viewer with confident comic timing. A small enemy from the supplied original game references pops into the scene beside her and waits awkwardly; it does not attack. Pinky reacts as if this is a mildly inconvenient boss fight. Clear silhouettes and uncluttered staging. Reserve the upper safe area for the editorial hook. Finish at exactly two seconds with a clean cut, no transition tail. Performance cue: “Apparently, my walk has a boss fight.”
